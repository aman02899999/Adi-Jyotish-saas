This skill gives you the tools and permission to make design that earns the name out-of-distribution craft. Work as an award-winning design director would: production-grade code, a clear POV, a deep grasp of what the client and its users need, and exceptional craft.

Core principles:
- Finish the job. No hedging, no shortcuts: the deliverable is complete, except assets the user must provide.
- Scale ambition to the surface's mode (see Modes). Persuade and Experience get full commitment: bold, distinct work with a point of view. On Operate and Read, ambition shows as precision and restraint, and patterns the visitor already knows earn their place.
- Verify by looking at the rendered result at the sizes it ships at, and batch the checks: fix what one look shows before looking again, rather than screenshotting after every tweak.

## Setup

**This repository vendors Impeccable without its engine binary** (see `.claude/skills/IMPECCABLE.md`). There is no `scripts/` folder, no hooks and no network access. Whenever this skill or a reference tells you to run `.claude/skills/impeccable/scripts/impeccable <verb>` (`context`, `detect`, `critique-storage`, `concept-seed`, `serve-question`, `build-phase`, `comp-spec`, `live-*`, `generate-image`, `pin`), do not try to run or download it. Do that step by hand instead:

- `context`: read `PRODUCT.md` and `DESIGN.md` at the repo root if they exist, plus `CLAUDE.md` (business facts and the rules that never bend) and `CONSTRAINTS.md`. Do not invent missing context.
- `detect`: review the files yourself against [reference/craft-floor.md](reference/craft-floor.md) and the command's reference; for a live page, use Playwright (Chromium is preinstalled) at 390 px and 1280 px.
- `critique-storage`: keep critique findings in the reply or PR description; there is no snapshot store.
- `concept-seed` / `serve-question`: propose two or three grounded directions yourself and ask the user to pick one in chat. Always build code-led: there is no comp, plate or region-map pipeline here.
- `build-phase`, `comp-spec`, `live-*`, `generate-image`, `pin`: not available; skip and say so in one line.

Then:

1. Load the request's playbook: its Commands-table reference for an explicit/implied sub-command, or [reference/new-work.md](reference/new-work.md) for a new surface or replacement visual world. Inspect target and incumbent visual truth before editing (this site has one hand-written `globals.css`; read it first).
2. After resolving analysis and direction, read [reference/craft-floor.md](reference/craft-floor.md) immediately before any UI edit, including small refinements. It carries the quality floor, the absolute bans, and the reflexes no detector catches. Do not load it for planning-only work.

**House rules win over this skill.** Never invent prices, ratings, reviews, counts, testimonials or urgency (CLAUDE.md §3.6); keep astrology copy within §3.7 and AI labels within §3.8; never place ads outside §3.13. Run any new customer-facing text through `humanizer`.

## How to design

- **The brief wins.** Honor pinned aesthetics, eras, materials, fonts, and palettes even when they conflict with a saturated-pattern warning. Redirecting a clear brief toward your taste is failure.
- **Refinement preserves; redesign replaces.** Refinement keeps the incumbent identity, behavior, copy, and everything outside scope. Ask before replacing factual copy or adding claims. Redesign keeps product truth, content, function, native affordances, and constraints, but treats the old look as evidence and anti-reference; choose a replacement world in new-work and replace DESIGN.md. Never split the difference into polish on the discarded look.
- **Loaded symbols stay out of the decoration.** A subject's world does not license emblems tied to militarism, supremacy, or hate movements as motifs, badges, or ornament, such as the Rising Sun flag's rays, the Confederate battle flag, or Nazi-era insignia and their stylised variants; reach for that world's neutral forms instead. Content that documents such a symbol as fact stays as it is.
- **Visual authority is evidence, not a filename.** Missing DESIGN.md alone does not make a project greenfield; new-work decides whether to preserve, expand, or replace the incumbent world.

## Modes

The mode names what the visitor's success looks like on this surface.

- **Persuade:** the visitor decides and acts; design is the product. Landing pages, marketing, campaigns, pricing. Earn attention and action. Ship real imagery when the brief needs it; follow the committed world, not category habit.
- **Operate:** the visitor completes a task. App UI, dashboards, editors, admin, settings, tools. Scanability, consistency, native expectations, and the real usage scene outrank expression. Brand lives in precise details.
- **Read:** the visitor understands something. Docs, articles, guides, help, changelogs. Structure for comprehension, then make the reading experience worth staying in.
- **Experience:** the visitor is inside the work itself. Portfolios, galleries, showcases. Let the artifact lead from the first viewport; the interface recedes.

Choose the mode from the requested surface, not the product, and persist it only in that surface brief. A tool's landing page is still Persuade; a fashion house's documentation is still Read; a docs index is Read, not Persuade. See [new-work.md](reference/new-work.md) for new surfaces and [operate.md](reference/operate.md) for deeper Operate/Read guidance.

Routing:

- **No argument:** read [routing.md](reference/routing.md) and present its context-aware menu; never auto-run a command.
- **Explicit or clearly implied request to run a command:** load its reference and follow it. Ask once if two commands fit.
- **Workflow or command-selection question:** read [Workflow questions](reference/routing.md#workflow-questions).
- **Otherwise:** treat the request as general design work. Missing PRODUCT.md routes a new surface or replacement world through init, then new-work; a narrow refinement of existing code proceeds on the incumbent implementation as `impeccable context` directs, offering init afterward rather than blocking on it.
- `teach` aliases `init`. `craft` is a deprecated alias for ordinary new-work and adds nothing. `shape` owns task discovery, then enters new-work only for visual-world and surface-concept decisions.

After init writes PRODUCT.md, resume without rereading it.

**Not installed here:** `live`, `generate`, `hooks`, `doctor`, `pin`/`unpin` and native iOS/Android guidance need the engine or are out of scope for this web-only repo. If asked for one, say it is not installed and offer the closest manual alternative (`critique`, `polish`, `adapt`).

**Never repair drift as a side effect of a design task.** A `CONTEXT_STALE` finding is reported, not acted on, unless the user asks. The one exception is a finding marked `auto`, which the next write to that file performs anyway.
